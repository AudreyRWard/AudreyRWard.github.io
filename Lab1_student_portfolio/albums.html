Albums

Alcohol ink on paper

This is an ongoing project of mine - dating back to early 2024. Every once in a while, I will get the motivation to add a few more drawings of album covers to this collection.

I start with a base 4x4 inch cardstock square of a base color - usually white, black, or the overwhelmingly major color of the image.

The albums are picked at random or by want at the time, usually completed in pairs of two artworks every few months. The idea was for this collection to span across vast mix of music I listen to, but that is ever-growing and I can't keep up.

Some of these albums I would not listen to anymore, or only ever liked one song on it, but it's my art and a part of this collection, so they all stay.
