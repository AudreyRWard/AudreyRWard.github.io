Hello, Heaven, Hello

Acrylic on canvas

In the song "Hello, Heaven, Hello", the musical artist YUNGBLUD goes through nine minutes pondering life in views of self expression, peer evaluation, and moving on to new stages.

This angel figure has just emerged from hell, after having had to treck through the depths before coming to a snowy field. But the journey isn't over; he still needs to get up the mountain, up to heaven.

He's been in hell for a while, and is trying to reach a new place. Not for anyone else. Just to grow in himself.
