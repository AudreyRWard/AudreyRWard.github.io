How are you so ok?

Acrylic on canvas

Relationships are hard. Sometimes you could be dying in silence and the other person has no idea. This painting shows much of my overthinking tendencies while my partner is on the opposite side of everything and is just happy when we write and OK by some silence in other times.

There's no right side to this. The blue person wants communication and feels isolated when there is time where the other comes off as too busy, just always OK, or is unaware of problems. The orange person lives in a state that their relationship, and life in general, is OK, and there's no bad thoughts circling his head, and he's just in love and doesn't realize the need to simply check in.

These two are tied together with the red string, stretching across their very different daily routine hours, and the big black silence line keeping them apart at times.
