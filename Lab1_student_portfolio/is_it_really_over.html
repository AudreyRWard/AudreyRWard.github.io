Is it really over?

Acrylic, cardboard on canvas

I dumped him. It's over, right? He's gone. I should be able to move on. Why am I still crying over him? This was my idea.

These strange thoughts get tangled around the little white figure (in the form of lyrics from songs) as they sit in the middle of space. Everything is spiraling around them, and it seems like there is no real fix.

Each time I saw him, it still hurt, even months later. If I wanted us to be done, so why did it keep hurting?

Painting is supposed to be my way of being done with something. Instead, this one kept drawing me back in to him.
