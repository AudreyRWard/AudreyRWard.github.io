Stay Here

Acrylic on canvas

For once, there's nothing demanding either of them to leave the bed.

It's the "make a wish" minute - 11:11. Their phones are put aside, though they describe the inner battles of them wanting to stay the night ("stay with me") but being unable to ("I can't").

His pillow that they share holds the secrets only the two of them will ever know.

They've got time. No rush, no classes, or work, or family. Just each other in his bedroom, wishing it could last longer.
