The Crash

Acrylic on canvas

Mental health can be a tough battle. When times get bad, it seems like it is inevitable that everything will all come crashing down.

At this point in time, I felt like I was driving but couldn't keep myself from harm.

The blood on the wheel - where the hands would grip, and in the center - shows how it feels to do something you know won't help. There's hazard lights and warning popups (like from a computer screen) warning the driver that they need to stop. But they can't slow down and find out what could happen.
