Alien Blues

Gouache on paper

"Alien Blues" - the short, few-worded indie rock song. It had few enough lyrics to be chosen for a zine, and the words were weird enough to create images from.

There's a relationship, but it's falling apart, and also sending the main character into self destruction - shown by drinking, and losing in court (top right), along with a lot of heated words (left of top right)
