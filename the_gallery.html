The Gallery

Acrylic on canvas

Sometimes it's the simple things that turn out to stick in your memory.

We didn't plan on visiting the gallery that night. But it was there where we met up, so we decided to go in. We're very familiar with each other but this was a new context for us. Walking around an art gallery. You have to be careful, and not too loud. It gives you the chance to stand closer, and lean in some while looking at the pieces.
